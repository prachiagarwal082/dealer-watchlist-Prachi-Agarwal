Assumptions and limitations
  • Data is synthetic: 268 dealers across four regions, generated with a fixed seed. Year-to-date means the first six months of the financial year.
  • Each signal is scaled 0 to 100 by its distance from a healthy level. The risk score is the weighted average. Critical is 55 or more and Watch is 35 to 54, applied to the unrounded score.
  • Attrition and audit scores are treated as early warnings of internal weakness, not proof of it.
  • Segments use K-Means with k-means++ starts, a fixed seed and 10 restarts. Names are set by rule: lowest average risk is Strong performers, highest is Distressed, and a segment with share of wallet above 80% is Exclusive partners. Everything else is Mixed signals.
  • Peers are the 5 nearest dealers by market potential, city tier, outlets and sales staff. They are matched on market and size, not on performance.
  • Key limitation: the data is driven by one underlying health factor, so segments mostly reflect severity. Real data would likely show more distinct problem types, and the weights should be validated against actual dealer outcomes.
